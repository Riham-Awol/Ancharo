About us

Why we're rebuilding Ancharo

A town is more than its buildings. It's the people, and the people of Ancharo are ready to build.

Our story

A place worth investing in

Ancharo sits among green highland hills. It's a town of markets, schools, mosques and churches, and of families who have lived here for generations.

Over the years, roads have worn down, schools have grown crowded and many streets go dark at night. Many of us who grew up here now live in other cities or abroad, but our hearts never left.

Rebuild Ancharo is a community-led campaign that connects everyone who loves this town with the projects it needs most.

How we work

Open, local and accountable

  • Transparent. We post every amount raised and spent on the news page.
  • Community-chosen. Residents and elders help decide which projects come first.
  • Local first. We hire local workers and buy local materials whenever possible.
  • Built to last. We'd rather finish one project properly than start five.
