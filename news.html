What's happening in Ancharo

Progress reports, community events, and where every birr is going.

Campaign1 November 2026

Rebuild Ancharo campaign officially launches

Community leaders, elders and youth came together in the main square to kick off the city's biggest renewal effort in years.

Read full story →Show less ↑

The campaign brings together residents and people from Ancharo who now live in other cities and abroad. Its first goals are repairing the main road, renovating schools, adding clean water points and installing solar street lights.

A volunteer committee will publish regular updates on this page showing how much has been raised and exactly how it is being spent.

Projects20 October 2026

Road repair: phase one is underway

Work has started on the first 2 km of the main road, the stretch that floods worst during the rainy season.

Read full story →Show less ↑

Crews are clearing drainage channels, filling potholes and laying a new surface. Local workers have been hired for the job, so the project also brings income into the town.

Phase two will continue toward the market once more funds are raised.

Community5 October 2026

Hundreds join the city clean-up day

Volunteers of every age cleaned streets, planted trees and painted the market walls in a single day.

Read full story →Show less ↑

Students, shop owners, elders and families turned out from early morning. By sunset, the main streets were cleared, hundreds of seedlings were in the ground and the market had a fresh coat of paint.

The next clean-up day will be announced here. Everyone is welcome.

Primary school renovation completed

Students returned to classrooms with a new roof, fresh paint and 80 new desks.

Read full story →Show less ↑

This was the first project fully funded by donations from the community. Thank you to every person who gave. You can see the result in our gallery.
