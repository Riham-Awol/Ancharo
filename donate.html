How to give

Three simple steps

  1. Choose an accountPick whichever bank or mobile money service is easiest for you.
  2. Send your donationAny amount helps. Add "Ancharo" as the reason or reference.
  3. Upload your receiptTake a screenshot of your receipt and upload it here. Once our team confirms it, it's added to the total and you get a thank-you certificate.
