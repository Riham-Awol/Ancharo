Where your money goes

Small gifts, lasting change

We focus on the basics that touch every family's day: getting around, learning, staying healthy and feeling safe at night.

Roads & paths

Repairing the main road and village paths so that markets, clinics and schools stay reachable in every season.

Schools

Fixing roofs, adding desks and books, and giving our children classrooms they're proud to learn in.

Clean water

Building and maintaining public water points so that no family has to walk hours for safe water.

Street lights

Solar street lights for safer evenings, longer market hours, and streets that feel alive after dark.

    About Ancharo

    A highland town with a big heart

    Surrounded by green hills and blessed with warm, hard-working people, Ancharo has always been a place where neighbours help neighbours.

    This campaign brings together people who live here and people who grew up here but now live far away. Together we can give the town the roads, schools and services it deserves.
